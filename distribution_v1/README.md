# distribution_v1 — rights-redacted distribution view

Rights transformation: `rights-1.0`. Generated by `scripts/inventory_rights.py`; counts are recomputed from the records on every run, never typed.

## What this view is

A redacted projection of the canonical layers. It carries this project's own metadata plus a pointer record for every piece of third-party expression that was removed. It is a **data-handling view**, not a legal opinion.

`METADATA_ONLY` is the **default data-handling policy** applied when no documented right to redistribute exists. 

> METADATA_ONLY is the default data-handling policy applied when no documented right to redistribute exists. It is not a determination that any transformed record is legally cleared for reuse or redistribution, and no record in this view should be read as legally cleared.

A sha256 and a permalink identify bytes. They do not grant reuse or redistribution rights. Nothing in this directory has been cleared by a rights holder, and no permission request has been granted.

## The release boundary

This directory is part of the released set. `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json` is the authoritative per-path statement of what may be redistributed; the paths it marks as withheld or as internal research artefacts are not licensed here, wherever they sit in the tree. **Attribution is not a redistribution right.**

## Every withheld pointer record carries

original source URL, immutable commit/report reference, file/page/line locator when available, source attribution, retrieval metadata, the sha256 of the actual original bytes, `status: WITHHELD`, the applied `rights_decision`, `transformation_version: rights-1.0` and the reason.

Short third-party prose at or below `200` characters may be carried as an attributed quotation and is marked `rights_decision: KEEP_FACTUAL_IDENTIFIER_WITH_ATTRIBUTION`. Nothing longer than that, and no third-party code of any length, is carried. A digest in `digests.jsonl` is a factual summary written by the VLUN authors from project metadata; it is marked `independently_written: true` and `copies_audit_prose: false`, and it is not a paraphrase of any audit text.
