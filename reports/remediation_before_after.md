## What was retracted rather than fixed

- The claim that 331 negatives had a defensible rejection reason: corroboration counted as explanation.

- The claim that a `corroborated_duplicate` record was established not to be a false positive wherever the pointer did not resolve to a published finding.

- The claim that every withheld citation keeps a sha256: the publisher-recorded digest exists on only part of them.

- The description of METADATA_ONLY as if it were a clearance, and the expectation that a permalink plus a hash grants reuse.

- Any implication that the held-out or sibling splits are contamination-proof; they are leakage-audited under the recorded criteria and no more.

- The label on the v2.1 API-sibling count of 71: `reports/api_sibling_stats.md` records that those were intra-contract pairs from `derived/counterexamples.jsonl`, not the cross-repository sibling call-site relation the name implies; the cross-repository family is built separately and reported at its own yield.

- The `forge_reason` field in the v2.1 experiment ledger. It was read from forge's human `[FAIL: …]` line, which `--json` does not print, so it was null on every recorded run even where the preserved stdout named the reason. The recorder is fixed, recorded artefacts were not rewritten, and the VLUN-Prove self-check says how many reasons it recovered by replaying those bytes.
