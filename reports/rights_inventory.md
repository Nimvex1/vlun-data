## Policy framing

It is not a determination that any transformed record is legally cleared for reuse or redistribution, and no record in this view should be read as legally cleared.

A sha256 and a permalink identify bytes; they do not grant reuse or redistribution rights.

## The release boundary

`RELEASE_MANIFEST.json` states, path by path, what may be redistributed. This report measures the content; the manifest decides the status of each path, and `scripts/check_rights_consistency.py` fails the build when the two disagree. **Attribution is not a redistribution right.**
