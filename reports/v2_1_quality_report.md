## Remaining blockers

- **archive RPC not distributed**  -  fork-based PoCs and the NOYA negative cluster  -  set VLUN_RPC_URL to a pinned archive endpoint and re-run scripts/run_verified_experiments.py
- **reconstruction ceiling on fixture drift**  -  4 declared reNFT experiments whose audited revision lacks a fixture the regression test needs  -  port the reporter's harness fixture by fixture; each failure is recorded with the missing symbol in experiments/*/vulnerable/result.json
- **judging rationale is not public**  -  negatives with no code-grounded reason and no corroborating finding  -  publish the judge or sponsor comments; 9 records already carry one
- **mitigation commits are cumulative**  -  patch delta classification  -  diff the single fixing hunk rather than the whole interval between the audited revision and the fix commit
